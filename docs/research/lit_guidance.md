# Historical Literature Survey: Test-Time Guidance

> Status update (2026-07-23): research note only. Current citations and Related
> Work are defined by `AAAI/main.tex` and `AAAI/references.bib`; current project
> claims are in `docs/PROJECT_STATE.md`.

**Scope**: Phase-1 research for style_residual_distill. Our method: FLUX.2 [klein] 9B (frozen, rectified-flow DiT), inference-time latent optimization (AdamW, ~5 inner iters / 25 denoise steps) driven by a **native same-state image-CFG score residual** (style-ref-conditioned score minus unconditional-image score, same noise state) as the style-transport direction. Our differentiator "carrier" (content self-affinity reweighting) is empirically inert. Goal: borrow a stronger training-free mechanism, single style ref, latent-only, zero training.

## 1. Method Table (anchor set, 2023–2025)

| Method | Venue / Year | Mechanism (1 line) | Signal used | Needs training? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Universal Guidance** (Bansal et al.) | CVPRW 2023 | Forward guidance (gradient of loss on predicted x̂₀ pushed into ε) + **backward guidance** (m-step inner gradient-descent to find Δx̂₀ minimizing loss, mapped back to ε-space) + **self-recurrence** (renoise-and-repeat k× per step) | Gradient of an off-the-shelf loss w.r.t. x̂₀ (Tweedie estimate) | No (guidance net can be pretrained/frozen; diffusion model untouched) |
| **FreeDoM** | ICCV 2023 | Builds a **time-independent energy function** from an off-the-shelf network (face/seg/style net) evaluated on x̂₀, backprops through denoiser to steer ε; adds a distance-based time-travel trick for stability | Energy/gradient from frozen auxiliary network on x̂₀ | No |
| **MPGD** (Manifold Preserving Guided Diffusion) | ICLR 2024 | Projects the guidance gradient onto the data manifold via a pretrained autoencoder's tangent space and applies it directly to the **clean-data estimate** (a "shortcut," not full multi-step backprop through the denoiser) — up to 3.8× faster, lower VRAM than DPS/FreeDoM/Universal Guidance | Gradient on x̂₀, manifold-projected via AE encoder/decoder | No |
| **PAG** (Perturbed-Attention Guidance) | ECCV 2024 | Replaces selected self-attention maps with the identity matrix (`Softmax(QKᵀ/√d) → I`), producing a structure-degraded "bad" prediction; guides away from it: `ε̃ = ε_θ + s·(ε_θ − ε̂_θ^PAG)` (same algebraic form as CFG) | Attention-map perturbation (identity substitution) | No |
| **SEG** (Smoothed Energy Guidance) | NeurIPS 2024 | Gaussian-blurs attention **queries** (equivalent to blurring the full attention matrix, but O(HW) not O(HW²)) to reduce the curvature of the self-attention energy landscape; combines additively with CFG: `dx ∝ (1−γcfg+γseg)s_θ + γcfg·s_θ(c) − γseg·s̃_θ` | Energy/curvature of self-attention, controlled by blur σ | No |
| **SAG** (Self-Attention Guidance) | ICCV 2023 | Aggregates self-attention maps → binary/soft mask of "attended" regions → adversarially Gaussian-blurs only those regions of the intermediate image → guides away from the blurred prediction, CFG-style | Self-attention-derived spatial mask + image-space blur | No |
| **CADS** | ICLR 2024 | Anneals the conditioning **vector** itself by adding scheduled, monotonically-decreasing Gaussian noise to it during sampling (not to the latent) to counteract mode collapse at high CFG scale | Noise injected into the condition embedding, timestep-scheduled | No |
| **CFG++** | ICLR 2025 | Reframes CFG as diffusion posterior sampling; the **only** change is the renoising step uses the **unconditional** noise estimate ε̂∅ instead of the guided one: `x_{t-1} = √ᾱ_{t-1}·x̂_c^λ + √(1−ᾱ_{t-1})·ε̂∅`, with λ∈[0,1] (small-scale regime). Demonstrated on DDIM/latent diffusion only — no flow-matching discussion in the paper itself. | Reordering of which score is used for renoising | No |
| **Autoguidance** | NeurIPS 2024 | `D_w = w·D_good + (1−w)·D_bad`, where D_bad is a genuinely **smaller-capacity and/or undertrained** sibling model (not the same frozen model degraded synthetically — the authors explicitly show synthetic degradations of a single frozen model, e.g. dropout/input-noise, **fail**) | Difference between two differently-*trained* models | **Yes** — disqualifying for us as literally stated; conceptually superseded by PAG/SEG/ERG/TPG which get a "free" bad-model via attention perturbation of the *same* frozen net |
| **Attend-and-Excite** | SIGGRAPH/TOG 2023 | Per-timestep **inner gradient-ascent loop directly on the latent** z_t (up to 20 steps to clear a threshold) minimizing `L = max_s(1 − max(A_t^s))` over cross-attention maps for neglected subject tokens; applied **only in the first 25/50 steps** (early steps fix spatial layout) | Cross-attention max-value loss, iterative latent gradient step | No |

## 2. Rectified-flow / DiT-specific and directly-adjacent downstream work

These came out of following the citation graph forward from PAG/SEG/Universal-Guidance and searching for flow-matching-specific guidance and training-free style transfer — several are unusually close to our exact setting.

| Method | Venue/Year | Mechanism | Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ERG** (Entropy Rectifying Guidance) | NeurIPS 2025 | Attention-mechanism perturbation generalized to **diffusion transformer** architectures; explicitly composable with CADS and APG; improves quality/diversity/consistency simultaneously rather than trading off | PAG/SEG-family idea ported to DiT — same architecture family as FLUX.2 |
| **TPG** (Token Perturbation Guidance) | NeurIPS 2025 | Applies a **norm-preserving shuffle** directly to intermediate token representations (not attention maps) — architecture-agnostic, needs no access to attention internals at all | Cheapest "bad-model" surrogate; least assumption on model internals, good fallback if FLUX.2's attention isn't easily hookable |
| **HeadHunter / SoftPAG** | NeurIPS 2025 | Searches individual attention **heads** (not whole layers) and finds specific heads governing structure vs. style vs. texture quality; **SoftPAG** linearly interpolates a selected head's attention map toward identity (continuous knob, not PAG's binary identity swap) — **validated directly on FLUX.1** for style-specific (text-prompted) guidance | Same model family as ours (FLUX). Style-controlling heads already identified for FLUX.1 — strong prior that FLUX.2 has an analogous head subset |
| **TAG** (Tangential Amplifying Guidance) | 2025 (arXiv 2510.04533) | Purely trajectory-based: decomposes *any* update vector Δ relative to the current (normalized) latent x̂ into normal (radial) `P·Δ` and tangential `(I−P)·Δ` components; amplifies **only the tangential part** by η≥1, leaving the radial/manifold-radius component untouched. Provably increases first-order log-likelihood gain. **Demonstrated on SD3 (flow-matching)**, no extra network evaluations | Zero extra forward passes; directly reusable as a post-hoc transform on any guidance vector we already compute, including our score residual |
| **FlowDPS / FMPS** | CVPR 2025 / arXiv 2411.07625 | Constructs a **surrogate score function** for flow-matching models (which lack an explicit score) by decomposing the flow ODE into a clean-estimate component and a noise component, injecting a likelihood-gradient correction into each — training-free posterior sampling for rectified flow. Demonstrated on linear inverse problems (inpainting/deblurring), not general T2I style transfer | Theoretical bridge confirming score-residual-style guidance is well-posed for rectified flow (relevant since our whole method rests on treating an image-CFG difference as a "score residual" in a flow model) |
| **Semantix** | ICLR 2025 (arXiv 2503.22344) | Training-free **single-reference** "Semantic Style Transfer": `ε̂_t = (1+ω)ε_θ(c) − ωε_θ(∅) + ∇_x[γ_ref·F_style + γ_c·F_spatial + γ_reg·F_semdist]`. Three energy terms: (1) style guidance via DIFT-style semantic correspondence between output and reference features, masked by k-means self-attention clusters; (2) spatial guidance — point-to-point cosine similarity between output and *content*-image features, preserving structure; (3) semantic-distance regularization via cross-attention swap + stop-gradient to avoid overfitting to structure. UNet SD backbone, DDPM inversion of both images, single style ref image (i.e., our exact problem framing, just different backbone) | The closest published analog to our task. Its "spatial feature guidance" term is essentially the content-preservation function our dead carrier was supposed to serve — but implemented as an explicit gradient term rather than a reweighting heuristic |
| **V-Shuffle** | arXiv 2511.06365 (Nov 2025) | Zero-shot style transfer: shuffles **value** vectors within self-attention to destroy semantic content while preserving low-level style statistics, plus a "Hybrid Style Regularization" for high-level style texture. Addresses content-leakage | Alternate "self-attention perturbation as a style/content decorrelator," but shuffles V not Q/K — different failure mode than PAG/SEG |
| **Z\*** | CVPR 2024 (arXiv 2311.16491) | Dual denoising paths (content, style) + **cross-attention rearrangement** to stop content/style blending, using vanilla SD without retraining | Older but establishes that cross-attention (not self-attention) swapping/rearranging is a viable content/style disentangling lever |
| **CFG-OEC** | arXiv 2511.14075 (Nov 2025) | Decomposes CFG's sampling error into a base term and a "cross term" from correlated conditional/unconditional errors; corrects the cross term via an **orthogonal decomposition**, using a model-prediction-based proxy for the (unobservable) ground-truth noise, dynamically stabilized across timesteps. Validated on SD1.5/SDXL only — no flow-matching demo found. | A candidate recipe for combining our style-residual guidance term with the base content-prompt CFG term without their errors constructively interfering — but unverified outside DDPM |

**Verification flags**: HeadHunter/SoftPAG's exact head-selection algorithm and SoftPAG's interpolation-coefficient formula could not be extracted from the abstract/project page (paywalled/JS-rendered PDF); mechanism above is stated at the level of confidence the sources support. CFG-OEC's precise orthogonal-decomposition formula likewise not extracted — treat the recipe below as directionally correct, verify against source before implementing. FlowDPS/FMPS have only been shown on linear inverse problems, not on prompt-driven T2I generation — extrapolation to our setting is our own inference, not a claim in those papers.

## 3. Concrete Borrowable Ideas

### Idea 1 — TAG-style tangential/radial decomposition of our own residual
**(a) Mechanism**: TAG decomposes any per-step update vector into a component parallel to the current (normalized) latent and a component perpendicular to it, then amplifies only the perpendicular ("tangential") part by a scalar η≥1, leaving the radial/norm-preserving part untouched. This provably increases first-order log-likelihood gain, i.e. pushes toward higher-density regions of the model's own manifold without changing the noise-schedule radius. It needs no extra network evaluation and is demonstrated on a flow-matching backbone (SD3).
**(b) Fold-in**: Take our existing style-transport residual `r = score(z_t | style_ref) − score(z_t | ∅)` (already computed for the CFG-style guidance term). Before adding `r` into the AdamW update, project it onto `x̂_t = z_t/‖z_t‖`: `r_∥ = (x̂_t·r)x̂_t`, `r_⊥ = r − r_∥`. Replace `r` with `r_∥ + η·r_⊥` (η starts at 1, sweep 1–3). Schedule: since TAG's guarantee is about likelihood/manifold-consistency, apply it every inner iteration, not just some timesteps — it's the cheapest possible modification (one dot product) and can be tried as a pure post-hoc wrapper around the current residual with no other code changes.
**(c) Expected effect**: Likely improves overall image naturalness/coherence (reduces artifacts introduced by pushing the latent off-manifold during optimization) — this is closer to a general quality/stability booster than a style-vs-content lever, so expect small-to-moderate gains on DINO-layout-leakage (fewer structural artifacts) with roughly neutral effect on CSD/style-sim unless η is pushed high.
**(d) Risk**: TAG's benefit is about likelihood, not about content/style disentanglement per se — it does not have a mechanism to know that our residual mixes style-transport with content-drift, so it may amplify content drift just as readily as style transport if that's the dominant tangential component. Should be tested against the specific failure mode the carrier was meant to fix (does it stop content bleeding, or just sharpen everything?).

### Idea 2 — PAG/SEG/HeadHunter-style attention-perturbation counter-term as a **replacement content-preservation force**
**(a) Mechanism**: PAG (identity-substituted self-attention) and SEG (Gaussian-blurred attention queries) both construct a structure-degraded ("bad") prediction of the *same frozen model* via one extra forward pass, then guide *away* from it exactly like CFG: `ε̃ = ε_θ + s·(ε_θ − ε_bad)`. Guiding away from a structure-degraded prediction pushes samples *toward more structure/coherence*. HeadHunter/SoftPAG refines this to specific attention heads and is validated directly on FLUX.1.
**(b) Fold-in**: Our carrier failed at reweighting by content self-affinity — a heuristic, hand-designed spatial mask. Replace it with a genuine second guidance term computed from the SAME frozen FLUX.2, no training: at each inner iteration, do one extra forward pass with selected self-attention heads perturbed (identity-substitute or query-blur, à la PAG/SEG; ideally search which heads via HeadHunter's per-head sweep, using our own CSD/DINO-layout metrics as the search objective instead of their text-concept criterion, since FLUX.1 head roles likely transfer approximately to FLUX.2). Form `struct_term = s_struct · (ε_θ − ε_bad_attn)` and add it alongside our style-residual `r`, i.e. combined update `Δz ∝ λ_style·r + λ_struct·struct_term`. Schedule λ_struct to decay over the 25 steps (structure is set early; per Attend-and-Excite's finding that spatial layout is fixed in the first half of steps) so it doesn't fight the style push once layout is locked.
**(c) Expected effect**: This directly targets what the carrier was supposed to do — hold content/structure steady while the style-residual pushes appearance — so this is the best-motivated candidate for recovering (or exceeding) the effect the carrier was designed for but didn't deliver. Expect improved DINO-layout-leakage / content preservation with a moderate risk of damping CSD style-sim if λ_struct is too high.
**(d) Risk**: Needs one extra forward pass per inner iteration (2× cost on that branch) — still training-free but not free computationally. Also, PAG/SEG were built and tuned for U-Net cross/self-attention; FLUX.2 is a DiT — HeadHunter/SoftPAG and ERG/TPG are the versions to actually copy (already ported to DiT/FLUX.1), not raw PAG/SEG. If FLUX.2's attention pattern differs enough from FLUX.1, the specific heads found for FLUX.1 style control may not transfer 1:1 and would need re-searching.

### Idea 3 — Early/late scheduling of the style-residual weight (à la Attend-and-Excite / CADS)
**(a) Mechanism**: Attend-and-Excite's iterative latent-gradient correction is applied *only in the first half of denoising steps*, because that's when spatial layout is determined; CADS anneals conditioning strength on a schedule across the trajectory to trade off diversity/fidelity.
**(b) Fold-in**: Zero new computation — just re-weight the existing residual's scale `s(t)` across the 25 steps and 5 inner iters. Two schedules worth an ablation: (i) *content-first*: down-weight style residual early (protect layout), up-weight late (texture/appearance is a late-step phenomenon) — this is the more standard hypothesis given rectified-flow literature (early steps = coarse structure, late = fine detail/texture, which is exactly where "style" usually lives); (ii) the literal Attend-and-Excite schedule (strong early, decaying late) for comparison, in case FLUX.2's rectified-flow schedule behaves differently from the DDPM U-Nets these papers were tuned on.
**(c) Expected effect**: If schedule (i) is correct for FLUX.2, expect content preservation to improve substantially for free, since content structure would no longer be perturbed by strong style pressure before it's resolved, while style fidelity (which lives in later high-frequency steps) is preserved or improved.
**(d) Risk**: Purely empirical — no paper directly establishes which half of a *rectified-flow* trajectory in FLUX carries layout vs. texture (Attend-and-Excite's finding is for a 50-step DDPM U-Net); this needs a cheap sweep, not a literature-guaranteed win. Also interacts with our own AdamW inner-loop step count — 5 inner iters is much shallower than Attend-and-Excite's up-to-20, so the schedule granularity may need adjusting.

### Idea 4 — Semantix's explicit 3-term energy as a template for a redesigned "carrier"
**(a) Mechanism**: Semantix runs single-reference training-free style transfer with three additive energy gradients: style (DIFT-style correspondence to reference, masked by k-means attention clusters), spatial (point-to-point cosine similarity to the *content* image's own features — the explicit content-preservation term), and a semantic-distance regularizer using cross-attention swap + stop-gradient to prevent over-fitting to structure.
**(b) Fold-in**: Rather than reweighting our style residual by a content self-affinity heuristic (the dead carrier), add Semantix's *spatial feature guidance* as a literal second loss term inside our AdamW objective: minimize `L_style(z) + λ·L_spatial(z)` where `L_spatial` = 1 − cosine-sim between DiT-block features of the current latent and of the (once-computed) content-image features at matched spatial positions. This gives content preservation a real gradient signal instead of a spatial reweighting mask.
**(c) Expected effect**: Directly targets content preservation with a differentiable term that composes naturally with our existing gradient-based (AdamW) optimization loop — more principled than the dead carrier's heuristic reweighting, and it is single-reference by design (matches our constraint).
**(d) Risk**: Semantix's full pipeline (DIFT correspondence + k-means clustering + cross-attention swap regularizer) is comparatively heavy — needs adaptation to DiT feature maps (Semantix's k-means/DIFT machinery was built for U-Net feature grids) and needs an extra forward pass on the content image per step to extract `F_c`. This is the most expensive of the four ideas and the correspondence/clustering machinery has not been shown on a flow-matching transformer.

---

## 4. Ranked "TRY FIRST" (cheapest first)

1. **TAG tangential/radial decomposition** (Idea 1) — a single projection on our already-computed residual, zero extra forward passes, already shown to transfer to a flow-matching backbone (SD3). Try this first as a pure wrapper.
2. **Timestep/inner-iter scheduling of the style-residual weight** (Idea 3) — also zero extra compute, pure hyperparameter/schedule sweep; cheap enough to run alongside Idea 1.
3. **PAG/SEG/HeadHunter-style attention-perturbation counter-term** (Idea 2) — one extra forward pass per inner iteration but is the most directly motivated replacement for the carrier's intended function (content/structure preservation), and HeadHunter/SoftPAG's finding that specific FLUX.1 heads control style is a strong, architecture-matched precedent.

Idea 4 (Semantix-style explicit content-preservation loss) is the most faithful reconstruction of "what the carrier should have done" but is the most expensive and least architecture-matched (built for U-Net); keep it as a fallback if 1–3 don't recover content preservation.
