# Title
Controllable Style Locality: one frozen-model score spear for semantic style editing and global style transfer

## Motivation
- Training-free style transfer with a frozen text-to-image transformer is usually treated as a single behavior — render the prompt's subject in a reference image's style. This flattens two genuinely different, useful tasks into one: (a) **Semantic Style Editing**, where the subject is painted in the reference's medium *and* keeps the reference's composition, with no mask or segmentation; and (b) **Global Style Transfer**, where the reference's composition is dissolved but its medium and palette are kept. Users want to *choose* where on this locality axis they land, not accept whatever a fixed method does.
- The two dominant routes to strong style transfer both carry a cost. (i) Distribution-matching distillation (DMD) trains a separate "fake-score" critic network to keep samples on the data manifold under strong guidance — this requires training and a second network at inference. (ii) Faithful-texture methods manipulate self-attention key/value statistics — an attention-level surgery that is architecture-invasive.
- The open question: can a **single frozen-model operator** — no weight training, no second network, no attention surgery — reach *both* ends of the style-locality axis, and can the locality itself be a *controllable knob* rather than a fixed behavior baked into the method?

## Method
1. **Velocity is conditional score (affine).** For a rectified-flow model there is an affine map between the predicted velocity and the conditional score, so a difference of velocities under two conditionings equals a difference of conditional scores, i.e. the gradient of a log-density ratio. Every velocity difference the frozen model emits is therefore an attractor we can move the latent along.
2. **Style guidance as the decoupled-DMD "spear."** Recast style guidance as the classifier-free-guidance augmentation term of decoupled distribution matching, `g = (α−1)(v_with − v_without)` — the reference-conditioned minus reference-free velocity — applied as a fixed-magnitude, rms-normalized pseudo-target step on the latent, with the model weights frozen and only the latent optimized.
3. **Regularization substitution (central claim).** In the single-sample, test-time, multi-step reverse-process regime, the frozen T-step reverse process *itself* is the distribution-matching regularizer. Consequently the trained fake-score "shield" of DMD is (i) **unnecessary** — the spear alone never collapses, even at large guidance — and (ii) **harmful** — a trained critic pulls samples back toward the natural-image manifold and erases structured media (e.g. mosaic tesserae). Dropping the shield and keeping only the spear yields **Test-time Score Distillation (TSD)**.
4. **Locality as one free knob.** Run the full spear over the full reverse trajectory to keep the reference composition = Semantic Style Editing; run *fewer* reverse steps to dissolve the reference composition while keeping the medium = Global Style Transfer. One operator, one continuous control along the style-locality axis, with mask-free subject preservation emerging rather than being enforced.
5. **No guidance-weight tuning.** Because the pseudo-target step is rms-normalized, the guidance scalar `α` washes out — only the spear's *direction* survives — so the method has essentially no guidance-weight hyperparameter; the live controls are reverse-step count and step size.
